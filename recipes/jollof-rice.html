Ghanaian Jollof Rice

Description

Ghanaian Jollof Rice is a popular West African dish prepared with rice, tomatoes, pepper, onions and a combination of delicious spices.

It is commonly served with chicken, beef, fish or fried plantain.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of rice
  • Fresh tomatoes
  • Tomato paste
  • Onions
  • Red pepper
  • Cooking oil
  • Garlic and ginger
  • Salt and spices
  • Chicken stock or water

Steps

  1. Wash and blend the tomatoes, onions and pepper.
  2. Heat cooking oil in a cooking pot.
  3. Add onions and tomato paste.
  4. Add the blended tomatoes and cook the stew.
  5. Add your spices and chicken stock.
  6. Wash the rice and add it to the stew.
  7. Cover and cook until the rice is soft.
  8. Serve with your preferred side dish.
